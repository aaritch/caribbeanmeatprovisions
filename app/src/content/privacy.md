## What we collect

When you submit a quote request, contact message, or newsletter subscription,
we collect the information you give us — including your name, company,
contact details, and any product or shipping information necessary to respond.

## How we use it

We use submitted information solely to respond to your inquiry, to manage your
account record, and — if you opt in — to send periodic news and special offers.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We may share it with service providers
(email delivery, hosting, anti-spam) under contractual confidentiality.

## Your choices

You can request a copy or deletion of your data, or unsubscribe from any
marketing email at any time.

## Contact us

For privacy questions, email **privacy@caribbeanmeatprovisions.com**.
